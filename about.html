Who We Are

Credible, Vetted & Registered Protection Solutions

Founded to fill the gap in high-reliability security services, MESJAT SECURITY LIMITED is a licensed protection firm operating for 2+ years across Kenya.

Headquartered in Bungoma town, MESJAT manages client risk factors by analyzing local vulnerabilities and matching them with modern operational defenses. We bridge the gap between human discipline (Manned Guarding) and electronic vigilance (CCTV & Alarm Systems) to maintain a complete safety shield.

All of our security guards undergo background vetting, standard health clearance, and rigorous drills before deployable placement. By keeping communications linked back to our operational control support facilities, we guarantee client facilities never remain isolated.
