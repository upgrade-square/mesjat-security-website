Sectors We Protect

Tailored Operations for Targeted Industries

Business & Corporate Security

Protecting physical transactions, intellectual capital, and retail operations.

Commercial workspaces face risks such as document theft, vandalism, and unauthorized intrusion. MESJAT implements strict visitor clearances, receptionist-guard integrations, and evening lock-up audits to maintain perimeter safety. In banking environments, we prioritize absolute vigilance, crowd control, and coordination with branch management.

Sectors Serviced:

▪ Corporate Head Offices
▪ Banks & Financial Hubs
▪ Retail Stores & Supermarkets
▪ Logistics Warehouses & Yards

Enterprise Protection Pillars

Access Audits: Vetted login rosters for all deliveries and guests.

Surveillance Checks: 24/7 CCTV tracking of warehouses and corridors.

Lock-Up Audits: Vested locking checks on all exterior doors after work hours.

Institutional Compliance

Asset Tracking: Logging institutional devices and equipment movement.

Emergency Coordination: Clear evacuation runs for student and patient centers.

Vigilance Protocols: Vetted verification checks during low-traffic night hours.

Public Sectors & Institutions

Meeting the strict compliance demands of healthcare, education, and NGOs.

Institutional systems operate under strict community-focused conditions. In schools and universities, protecting student populations and limiting entrance access points to verified visitors is primary. In healthcare clinics, our guards are trained in compassionate redirection, ensuring emergency vehicle lanes remain clear and operations are not delayed by security bottlenecks.

Sectors Serviced:

▪ Schools & Universities
▪ Hospitals & Health Clinics
▪ Government offices
▪ NGOs & Agency headquarters
▪ Churches & Worship halls

Property, Estates & Residential Protection

Securing families, residential compounds, hotels, and builder assets.

Residential estates, apartments, and hotels require security systems that guarantee safety. We secure access gates, monitor perimeter electric wiring, and carry out regular patrol walks through darker areas of properties during night shifts. For construction locations, our guards log materials, preventing tool thefts and property trespassing.

Sectors Serviced:

▪ Residential Estates & Gates
▪ Apartment buildings
▪ Private Residential Holdings
▪ Hotels & Guest apartments
▪ Construction & Building sites

Community Security Pillars

Visitor Verification: Direct phone clearances with residents before allowing gate entry.

Wire Soundness: Scheduled visual sweeps of wall spikes, heights, and security cables.

Materials Auditing: Standard verification checklists on construction site assets.

Specialized Deployments

Discreet bodyguards: Vetted suit-and-tie close escort details.

Risk pre-analysis: Pre-checking transport lanes and hotel access structures.

Perimeter fencing: Structured coordination of barriers during open-air crowds.

Events & Specialized Protection

Securing high-profile gatherings, transiting dignitaries, and VIPs.

Events must proceed smoothly under high visitor load. We handle security controls, bag screening, credential checks, and exit logistics. For executives or government delegates, our dedicated close protection bodyguards manage safety details without distracting clients.

Sectors Serviced:

▪ Corporate Conferences & Annual Meetings
▪ Civic & Community Assemblies
▪ Private VIP Escorting Operations
▪ Political & Diplomatic Delegations
