Comprehensive Security Operations Scope

1. Manned Guarding / Static Security

Disciplined physical protection presence on-site, 24/7.

Our baseline service. MESJAT deploys properly vetted and trained static security guards to secure entry checkpoints, visitor logs, and perimeter fences. Suitable for corporate headquarters, banks, residential estates, private households, logistics depots, and healthcare facilities.

We prioritize drill-trained behavior, absolute attendance, and strict compliance with local safety standards. Guard activity is monitored via scheduled supervisor visits and real-time control room radio contact.

2. Mobile Patrols

Randomized vehicular surveillance checks for complete deterrence.

For locations that do not require constant guarding presence or require additional outer perimeter support. MESJAT marked patrol vehicles carry out regular, randomized visits to customer sites, checking padlock integrity, perimeter sensors, and general activity.

Our mobile units remain on standby to assist static guards, verify alarms, or support key-holding services when quick response is necessary.

3. Event Security

Safety design, access checkpoints, and traffic flow coordination.

Large-scale public shows, corporate banquets, high-profile weddings, or community programs require specialized crowd management. MESJAT plans and coordinates logistics pathways, baggage screenings, perimeter checkpoints, and physical escort Details during gatherings.

We work in collaboration with organizers to prevent unauthorized access and maintain safety without causing disruption to guests.

4. CCTV Monitoring & Surveillance

Modern electronic vigilance connected directly to dispatcher control.

We combine physical control rooms with remote camera integrations. MESJAT operators monitor live video feeds to detect suspicious activity immediately. Early identification allows our team to notify field officers, preventing losses before intruders reach building entries.

Suitable for warehouses, banks, shopping complex pathways, and residential community common roads.

Discuss Surveillance Systems

5. Alarm Response & Key Holding

Secure key custodial holding and immediate transmitter dispatch.

Under alarm response, MESJAT operates a secure key-holding service. In the event of system alerts, our control room immediately dispatches armed-support coordinate patrol units to access the premises and inspect the interior safely.

This avoids client staff being called out during dangerous night hours, ensuring licensed professionals manage alerts.

Enquire About Alarm Plans

How Key Holding Operates

Step 1: Client keys are sealed in tamper-proof bags inside our secure vault.

Step 2: Sensor alert is transmitted to our operations center.

Step 3: Mobile officers access keys and inspect internal premises safely.

MESJAT offers specialized protection details for business executives, delegates, or high-net-worth individuals. Deployed officers are highly trained in threat identification, route risk mapping, close escort drills, and emergency evacuation maneuvers.

We balance rigid safety requirements with complete professional discretion, ensuring our clients can conduct business with minimal distraction.
