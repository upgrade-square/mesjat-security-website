Why Partner With Us?

Our Operational Strengths & Assets

Operational Vetting

Trust Built on Compliance, Vigilance & Accountability

We differentiate ourselves through strict administrative compliance and responsive support frameworks.

Too often, security guarding details are treated as an informal transaction. At MESJAT, we manage guard selection, drill updates, and surveillance coordination as structured operations. Our client sites in Bungoma and across Kenya have direct access to support through our control operations rooms, ensuring that help is always available.

Every security guard deployed is fully vetted, ensuring that customers are protected by reliable, qualified, and disciplined security personnel.

1. Regulatory Compliance & Licensing

MESJAT SECURITY LIMITED holds full registration with the relevant Kenyan private security regulatory authorities. We adhere to local labor law compliance, vetting routines, and training benchmarks to ensure high-performance standards.

2. Full Insurance Liability Cover

We protect our clients against unexpected liabilities. MESJAT maintains complete insurance liability cover to protect your business premises, properties, assets, and operations while our teams are on duty.

3. 24/7 Operations Control Support

Duty guards remain connected back to our operations control center. Continuous check-ins and emergency call buttons are integrated, preventing site personnel from encountering difficulties without support.

4. Mobile Patrol Vehicles Support

MESJAT operates dedicated, marked patrol cars. These vehicles support static guards, carry out supervisor assessments, and provide rapid backup support for properties when required.

5. Qualified Guarding Personnel

All guards undergo standardized security drills, emergency response training, and background vetting. Vetting information is held securely, assuring clients of complete accountability on their properties.

6. Custom System Integrations

We design security systems specifically for your facility. By combining gates, CCTV, panic alarms, and patrol checks, we deliver specialized security solutions that match your budget and risk profile.
